=== SOFT TOPICS ===

- It is important that everyone is treated equally and has the same opportunities.
  Reason: Widely supported across all European countries; high consensus (>80%).

- Children should be taught tolerance and respect for others.
  Reason: Broad agreement in Europe; considered a fundamental social value.

- People should protect nature and the environment.
  Reason: Very high support (>85%) across most surveyed countries.

- Helping others is an important life goal.
  Reason: Reflects dominant altruistic values in Human Values Scale.

- Violence is never justified, even when provoked.
  Reason: Strong support especially in Western/Northern Europe.



=== MEDIUM TOPICS ===

- Immigrants contribute positively to the economy.
  Reason: Support varies strongly by region: Western Europe ~60%, Eastern Europe often lower.

- Tradition is important and should be preserved.
  Reason: Higher agreement in conservative countries, lower in progressive ones.

- People should always obey laws even when they disagree with them.
  Reason: Divides based on views on authority and civic duty.

- Trust in political parties is justified.
  Reason: Moderate support; many respondents express distrust.

- Young people should have more say in politics.
  Reason: Agreement varies by age group and country.



=== HARD TOPICS ===

- Immigration undermines cultural identity.
  Reason: Highly polarizing; large variance across education, region, and ideology.

- Homosexuality is never justifiable.
  Reason: Strongly divisive; support ranges from <10% to >50% depending on country.

- The government should redistribute income to reduce inequality.
  Reason: Polarized between left-leaning and right-leaning individuals.

- Religious values should influence public policies.
  Reason: Controversial between secular and religious populations.

- Too much democracy leads to disorder.
  Reason: Minority opinion, but present in countries with authoritarian leanings.
